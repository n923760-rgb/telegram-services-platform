# Bilingual CV in one document

## Authority and baseline
Owner selected Arabic-English inside the same file, requested market evidence before
implementation, then explicitly authorized implementation and merge. Baseline main
0d42d1dfeb4175ae36ded0d4ec31b727c777f510 / CV v2.

## Product contract
CV v3 offers Arabic, English or bilingual in one editable Word and corresponding PDF.
Bilingual intake collects final Arabic content followed by a conditional English form.
Both bodies must independently supply name, contact, skills and education or experience.
The four purpose profiles remain; their section ordering applies to both bodies.
Each supplied section is rendered Arabic then English in one single-column document.
Optional sections supplied only in one language remain present, without manufacturing
a counterpart. Names appear together in the title; original source text stays literal.
No translation or claims of semantic equivalence of independently supplied bodies.

## Safety and scope
Existing generic form/when intake, script/date builder and saved-pair delivery reused.
English fields are inactive for monolingual requests and rejected if supplied there.
Per-language 9000-character and per-field/line limits retained, up to 18000 combined.
No service-specific bot/core branching, provider calls, new engine, dependency,
migration, price/activation changes or historical cost settlement. Provider pause stays
untouched. Unprepared stale v2 work follows existing version cancellation/release;
prepared artifacts follow existing replay recovery. No licensed seller assets copied.

## Limits
Market evidence proves advertised paid offers, not verified transactions or popularity.
No universal ATS certification, automated translation, dual-column layout or forced
one-page output. Sample bodies span two pages without truncation. Generic preview
shows the first page and page count; it is not a full multilingual semantic review.
